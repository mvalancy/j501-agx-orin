What changed in Rev 0.9

  • The cooler is the one actually fitted: Seeed's reComputer mini J501 heatsink with fan (S05/S06), a 2 mm copper spreader, a G48R tower with five 6 mm heat pipes and a 92 × 15 mm, 12 V fan, 50.3 mm tall above the thermal pad. The fan is centred on the module (the user's photo: its four ears sit on the module's mounting holes). Rev 0.8 had assumed the 34 mm WS-RDN-601, and its printed lid hit this cooler by about 15 mm.
  • Enclosure 97 mm tall (Rev 0.8: 92 mm, Rev 0.7: 112 mm). The lid is cut away over the fan down to a flush 3 mm grille (12 spokes, 2 rings): the fan top is 2.4 mm under it, so the enclosure is only as tall as the cooler needs.
  • Skirt around the fan frame (2.4 mm wall, 6 mm clear): the fan blows straight out through its grille, cool air comes in through the wall vents and the hex cells further out (the blind band next to the skirt keeps them away from the plume), and the cut-out is stiffened; the star ribs start on it, with round bosses at the joints.
  • Recovery riser for a printed Rev 0.8 lid (tools/lid_riser.py, 18 mm, 4 × M3 × 30): not a release part.

Assumptions and limits
  • steady RANS (k-omega SST): natural-convection plumes really fluctuate
  • no radiation: passive cases are pessimistic (radiation can carry a third of the heat from a hot plate in still air)
  • walls use wall functions without prism layers, so wall heat transfer depends on the mesh: check a case one level finer before trusting a small difference
  • converged means the temperatures, fan flow and heat leaving stopped changing and the heat leaving matches the heat put in (energy balance), not that the residuals reached a tolerance
  • isotropic solids: heat pipes are an effective conductivity, with no dry-out limit
  • obstacles (enclosure, boards) are adiabatic: the heat leaves only with the air
  • fans are actuator discs with a straight-line fan curve, no swirl

With the cooler at 40 W

Rev 0.8 was simulated with the 34 mm Seeed WS-RDN-601 (80 mm, 5 V fan under a lid with an intake ring). Rev 0.9 uses the cooler actually fitted, the reComputer mini J501 heatsink: a 2 mm copper spreader, a five-heat-pipe G48R tower and a 92 mm, 12 V fan whose top sits 2.4 mm under a flush grille in the roof. The two columns compare enclosure and cooler together. The CFD resolves the enclosure, vents, grille, skirt and cooler body; the fan is an actuator disc on its datasheet curve; the thin fins are a porous block whose resistance comes from the plate-fin model.

Plate temperature = the fin temperature at which the fins carry 40 W in the CFD + 40 W × (spreading + base, cadagent.heatsink) + 40 W × pad. A plate-fin model alone gives 45.0 °C (Rev 0.8) / 31.9 °C (Rev 0.9). Rev 0.9 fins: 0.35 mm plates at 1.75 mm pitch (pitch counted from a photo, thickness assumed). The fan runs its full-speed curve; the board's fan control will run it slower when cool.

Topology optimisation

pyMOTO: minimum compliance (stiffest layout) for a volume budget, SIMP with a density filter and an overhang filter for support-free printing, solved with MMA. Features that have a job (posts, columns, feet, joints) are kept solid; the optimiser decides the rest. The result is read into the parametric CAD, not printed directly.
